# Cold email and the law (read this, it's short)

This is not legal advice. Rules differ by country and change; check what
applies to you.

- **EU / UK (GDPR, PECR, ePrivacy):** B2B cold email to named work
  addresses is commonly sent under "legitimate interest", but some
  countries (e.g. Germany) are stricter about unsolicited email. Keep it
  relevant to the person's job, say who you are, honour every "no" at once,
  and be ready to say where you got their address (the `source_url` in
  `leads.csv` is your record). Delete people's data when they ask.
- **US (CAN-SPAM):** no misleading headers or subjects, identify yourself,
  include a valid postal address, and honour opt-outs within 10 business
  days.
- **Everywhere:** low volume, real one-to-one emails, and a quick, polite
  answer to "please remove me" keep you safe and keep your domain off spam
  lists.

The kit helps by only using published addresses with a recorded source,
sending nothing automatically, closing leads after the last follow-up, and
treating every "no" as final. Add an opt-out line to the playbook if your
market requires one.
