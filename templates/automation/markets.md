# Market rotation

PROSPECTING.md picks one market per run. `core` markets are due every 14
days, `expansion` markets every 42 days, and expansion markets only run when
no core market is due. Edit the table to add, remove or re-tier markets.
`last_run` and `total_added` are updated by the runs; leave `last_run`
empty for a new market.
